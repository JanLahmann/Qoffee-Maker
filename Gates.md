# Description of the Quantum Gates <img src="Bilder/QuoffeeMug_vector.png" alt="" width="150" height="150">

"Quantum Gates" are the fundamental operations that can be applied to Qubits.  
The three most important Quantum gates are NOT, CNOT and H.  
For more details see [Operations glossary](https://quantum-computing.ibm.com/composer/docs/iqx/operations_glossary).

<img src="Bilder/NOTgate.png" alt="NOT gate (Pauli X): flips a qubit from 0 to 1 and from 1 to 0" width="500" height="187">

<img src="Bilder/CNOTgate.png" alt="CNOT gate: flips the target qubit only when the control qubit is 1" width="500" height="281">

<img src="Bilder/Hadamardgate.png" alt="Hadamard (H) gate: turns 0 or 1 into an equal superposition of 0 and 1" width="500" height="281">
